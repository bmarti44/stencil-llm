## Findings

1. **Critical — The registered M1b-init damped-energy fixture squares the envelope twice and cannot pass a correct implementation.** Phase 2 `test_damped_energy_decays` regime (b) requires, at `G = softplus(-9)`, "the `H_10000 / H_0` ratio within [0.8, 1.2] x `(1+G)^(-20000)` (~0.30) — the modal envelope the map's determinant implies" (PLAN.md:359, "Phase 2. Models and plumbing proofs"). The formula and the parenthetical disagree, and the plan's own determinant reasoning contradicts the written exponent. The homogeneous one-step map of the registered discretization (Section 5.2) has determinant `(1+G)^(-1)` per step (verified symbolically: `det = (1/(1+G))(1 - A/(1+G)) + A/(1+G)^2 = 1/(1+G)`); underdamped eigenvalue magnitudes are `det^(1/2)`, so a quadratic energy decays as `det^k = (1+G)^(-k)`. With `G = 1.23402e-4` and `k = 10000`, the correct envelope is `(1+G)^(-10000) = exp(-1.2339) ≈ 0.2911`, matching the "(~0.30)" note; the written `(1+G)^(-20000) = exp(-2.468) ≈ 0.0847` gives the interval [0.0678, 0.1017], which excludes every faithful implementation of the registered map. An implementer who follows the formula fails a correct cell; one who follows "~0.30" silently violates the written threshold. Gate G2 is not honestly passable until the exponent, energy functional, expected value, and tolerance are made mutually consistent. (Independent concurrence with sol science#20 / spec#3; sol's algebra is correct.)

2. **High — H4's 8.25% boundary is a G3.1-style margin mislabeled as a statistical detection threshold, and mean-over-seeds banding can conceal a decisive per-seed side-channel.** The v1.6 amendment log says H4 bands were "re-cut at the detection threshold (8.25)", and Appendix C's G3.6 H4 row reads "<= 8.25 (null + 2, the detection threshold) reads as no detected routing (not proof of purity)" (PLAN.md:483). But Task M's null is 6.25% with "Metric is exact-match accuracy pooled over all answer-decision positions" (PLAN.md:320) — 8 answers x 10,000 sequences. Even under the most adverse assumption of perfect within-sequence correlation (effective n = 10,000), the binomial standard error is `sqrt(0.0625*0.9375/10000) = 0.242 pts`, so 8.25% sits ≈8.2 standard errors above null: routing is statistically decisive far below the "no detected routing" boundary. Separately, the row aggregates by "mean over seeds per variant": seed accuracies [12, 6.25, 6.25] mean 8.17 and read "no detected routing," although the 12% checkpoint alone is ~24σ evidence that the trained architecture can route content. Since H4 exists precisely to police the strict control/content separation claim (Section 1, H4: "high beyond-window Task M accuracy ... forces the strict control/content separation claim ... to be withdrawn or qualified"), a false purity story can survive every mandatory gate. Detection must be a per-seed statistical test against 6.25%; 8.25 may remain only as a separately labeled practical-effect cutoff. (Concur sol science#15; numbers independently recomputed and confirmed.)

4. **Medium — The H2 decision rule bundles the explicitly-uncontrolled M1-vs-B2 contrast into support/refute of the dissipation hypothesis.** H2 (Section 1) first quarantines B2: it "differs in recurrence order, depth, and nonlinearity, not only in energy handling — and its results are read as 'what a simpler fading-memory architecture achieves,' never as a third point on a controlled dissipation axis." The adjacent pairs of "M1b >= M1 >= B2" are (M1b,M1) and (M1,B2). Consequences: (a) H2 can be declared REFUTED solely because B2 — a comparison the plan says carries confounds — beats M1, even when the clean M1b>M1 contrast supports learned dissipation; (b) a clean M1b>M    1 result with B2≈M1 (gap < 3) lands "inconclusive," withholding "supported" from the controlled contrast because of the uncontrolled one. The estimand the hypothesis is actually about is M1 vs M1b; the B2-adjacency term should be report-only, or H2's support/refute sentences should be rewritten so only the (M1b,M1) adjacency adjudicates the dissipation claim.
