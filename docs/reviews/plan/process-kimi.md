(Calibration divergence from sol#1 acknowledged, not a factual dispute.)

23. **Low — NEW (observed in round-1 evidence but unfiled there; a clear in-scope miss): the session-continuity audit note is undeliverable.** Review-session continuity promises an unusable-session fallback "noted in the round log". Verified in `tools/run_codex_review.sh`: `RESUME_NOTE` is constructed from `${SID:-}` in the prompt-build block BEFORE `SID` is read from the session file in the argument-build block, so the resume/anti-churn note is always empty even on successful resumes; and when the resume fails, the retry reuses the already-built `PROMPT` unchanged — no flag informs the fresh reviewer that continuity was lost. The fallback now functions (retry implemented, v1.7), but its mandated audit trail cannot be produced, and the round log will falsely imply uninterrupted sessions. Fix: read `SID` before prompt construction and inject an explicit fallback flag into the retry prompt. (Concur sol process#24.)

24. **Low — NEW (rubric-named budget-realism check missed in round 1): Phase 5's 24 GPU-hour budget ignores context-length scaling and will likely trip the 2x pause.** Phase 3 anchors at 96 runs / 72 GPU-hours (~0.75 h/run) at Task A/M sequence lengths (~2.1k tokens, window 64). Phase 5 trains 27 runs (3 variants × 3 seeds × R∈{2,8,32}) whose R=32 sequences run ≈8.3k tokens (32 × ~260) — roughly 3–4x the per-step cost — with the same 20k steps. Blended across R∈{2,8,32} the mean sequence is ~3.5k (~1.7x), so a Phase-3-anchored projection lands ~35–80 GPU-hours against a 24-hour budget, i.e., 1.5–3.4x. The budget-discipline rule ("A matrix projected within 2x the phase's stated budget may launch ...; beyond 2x, pause for the human") catches this at pilot time, so no science is at risk — but a budget predestined to trigger a human touchpoint is a planning defect, and the R=32 cell alone may exceed 2x even when the blend does not. Register a per-R cost model in the Phase 5 pilot (Section 2b budget discipline) rather than reusing the Phase 3 anchor blindly.
