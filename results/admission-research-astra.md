# Standing-instruction admission: research and decision — 2026-09-06
Live primary-source research, 2023–2026; gpt-6-astra. Research only: no model launched, GPU accessed, process signalled, or sealed/benchmark input read; only this report written.

**Recommendation:** stop sentence-head enrichment for now. Run one frozen Qwen3-1.7B message-level, evidence-preserving extraction experiment; retain the relation/register machinery. This is a better diagnostic formulation, not a demonstrated high-precision replacement. I found no published production result certifying the specific standing-rule-versus-request/quotation boundary.

**Local diagnosis.** [V8](quick-checks/focus3-gate/v8/RESULTS.md) has 11 false admissions in 96 replay records: eight payload requests and three inert quotes; 96 is a record denominator, not either family's negative support. A further completion acts on an already polluted row.
The reused Fable diagnostic yields admission precision 111/(111+8)=93.28%, recall 111/124=89.52%, and non-rule admission 8/239=3.35%. These measure the older rule taxonomy, not a fresh message-extraction task.
V8 already uses sentence-plus-context paired inputs; merely adding context is not the untried fix. Its decision/output unit, three-way labels, 192-token training bound and family sampling remain limitations. Zero errors on 21 DEV negatives has a one-sided 95% binomial upper bound of 13.29%, before allowing for family dependence.

Production synthesis: future usefulness, explicit commitment, repetition, speaker provenance, domain/schema restrictions and temporal scope all appear. These systems generally store useful facts as well as instructions; copying their broad “remember useful information” prompts would reproduce Stencil's category error.

**2. Detection formulations: retain message context and separate semantic questions**

| Formulation | Evidence-backed advantage | Expected failure on this task / decision |
|---|---|---|
| Sentence classification | Existing BGE head is cheap and has measured local recall. | Sentence labels collapse authority, lifetime, act and scope; context can be truncated or outside the selected span. Requests/quotes can look like directives. Reject another small same-author enrichment as the next experiment. |
| Message-level structured extraction | LangMem/CarMem/VARS demonstrate conversation-to-structured-memory pipelines; can emit zero, one or several source-backed rules with scope. | Models may obey quoted text, infer durable preference from one task, merge scopes or fabricate spans. JSON guarantees syntax only; schema complexity also consumes small-model capacity. Preferred experiment, with abstention and exact source validation. |
| Pairwise/contrastive scoring | Our relation branch demonstrates useful target-conditioned discrimination. [BGE's authors](https://huggingface.co/BAAI/bge-small-en-v1.5) describe contrastive training, cross-encoder reranking and dataset-dependent similarity thresholds. | Similarity to “standing instruction” measures semantic resemblance, not user adoption or duration. Full-message cross-encoding of matched request/rule and report/adoption pairs is a plausible later training objective; no located direct precision result warrants using cosine prototypes as admission. |
| NLI/entailment | [DeBERTa zeroshot v2, 2024](https://huggingface.co/MoritzLaurer/deberta-v3-base-zeroshot-v2.0) explicitly supports text+hypothesis entailment and evaluates 28 classification tasks. | Use premises containing the whole message/context and hypotheses about the *current user's commitment*, not merely “contains an instruction.” Evaluate authority, continuation beyond the immediate reply, and scope separately. Generic NLI benchmarks do not certify these pragmatic distinctions; hypothesis wording and truncation matter. |

A useful NLI hypothesis is “The current user adopts the selected constraint for subsequent work on this artifact or future replies.” Competing hypotheses cover a one-result operation and inert reported/quoted material. Preserve absolute entailment/non-entailment scores; a forced softmax over only competing positive descriptions must choose something even when none applies.
An adopted quote can be a rule; a quote merely being translated, criticized, logged or attributed to someone else cannot. Data presence is neither a veto nor a positive signal. The semantic question is whether the user commits the assistant to an ongoing constraint independently of producing the immediate result.
[Instruction Hierarchy, 2024](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.13208) supports explicitly training privilege distinctions, but does not establish a detector for quotes embedded inside a user turn. Delimiters and role labels help represent provenance; they do not guarantee that a small extractor respects it.
[Constraint Tax, May 2026](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.26128v1) reports schema validity rising 61.5%→100% while answer accuracy falls 19.7%→11.0% across its small-model tasks. This is not memory extraction, but rules out treating constrained JSON as semantic validation.

**3. Does the local corpus suffice, and what does inference cost?**

The v8 admission pool is 20,934 sentence/context rows, already including the 300 request/rule additions (200 negatives, 100 positives). Do not add that 300 twice. The [relation report](relations-classifier-report.md) counts 7,749 *pairs*, not 7,749 independent new-rule messages.
[LABELS-RELATIONS.md](../data/classifier/LABELS-RELATIONS.md) already provides the right distinction: explicit message_new_rule plus source spans, and old_rule=null admission items. Audit those fields after patches and deduplicate by message/scenario before claiming available extraction support. A relation-none pair may contain an unrelated new rule; cancellation/reinstatement may be old-taxonomy rule without being new-key admission.
The relation pool also discloses 90 evaluation-derived Astra relatives and repeated held-out use. Exclude contaminated relatives from a new clean admission fit/demo pool; preserve the old lineage disclosure for the unchanged relation branch. This research did not read held-out examples or recount raw corpora.
**Verdict:** enough material to bootstrap/reannotate a message/span classifier; not evidence that a high-precision extractor can be trained as-is. BGE-small is a 384-dimensional, 512-token encoder, not a JSON decoder. It can learn admission/span/scope heads with aligned supervision; arbitrary rule text or semantic keys do not emerge from replacing the three-way head. [BGE model card](https://huggingface.co/BAAI/bge-small-en-v1.5)
First adjudicate a contract tension: [LABELS.md](../data/classifier/LABELS.md) says mixed request+writing-constraint is rule, explicitly including “Summarize this in under 100 words.” Do not silently make every payload-bearing request negative. Distinguish an operation on supplied data from an ongoing artifact constraint; retain the registered task-scoped default where applicable and annotate unresolved scope as ambiguous.
A frozen instruction-tuned [Qwen3-1.7B](https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen3-1.7B), with a few contrastive demonstrations, is the fastest way to test whether general discourse understanding repairs the boundary without a new fit. Prefer 1.7B for this experiment; choosing 0.6B solely for speed risks repeating the capacity problem. A small seq2seq model still needs the missing aligned extraction supervision.

| Cost item | Defensible estimate / limitation |
|---|---|
| Added weights | Approximately 3.4 GB for 1.7B parameters at two bytes, versus 1.2 GB for 0.6B; FP32 doubles these. Ideal four-bit payloads are 0.85/0.30 GB before quantization metadata and runtime state. Quantization changes require their own validation. |
| Local per-message charge | No API token fee; CPU/GPU time, memory and power remain real costs. No model was timed in this research. One decoder call replaces repeated admission-span calls; relation comparisons retain their existing cost. |
| CPU latency | T ≈ input_tokens/prefill_rate + output_tokens/decode_rate + grammar/validation overhead. Illustrative, **unmeasured** rates of 128–512 input and 10–40 output tokens/s give 3.6–14.4 s for 1,024 input+64 output tokens. These are planning assumptions, not measured host/model speeds. Empty outputs can be much cheaper. |
| Published GPU anchor | [Qwen speed tests](https://qwen.readthedocs.io/en/latest/getting_started/speed_benchmark.html): H20 96GB, batch 1, BF16 Qwen3-1.7B, input length 1: 59.83 tokens/s with Transformers, 227.80 with SGLang. Their metric counts input+output over 2,048 generated tokens; it is **not** short-message latency and cannot certify our one-hour budget. |

**4. One proposed admission design and one stop/go experiment**

Use one frozen Qwen3-1.7B sidecar inside generate(), before trunk prefill, with thinking disabled, deterministic decoding and a compact constrained schema. It selects evidence from the whole new message plus previous user context and existing user-visible task bindings; it has no tools or write access. Register mutation remains ordinary validated code.
Contract: return {"rules":[]} or up to four candidates containing {rule, scope, key, evidence, attribution}. rule and evidence are verbatim substrings; scope is conversation or a visibly grounded task/artifact; attribution is direct or explicitly adopted. key is an existing runtime key or NEW, never a free-form guessed identifier.
Admission requires supported user adoption, persistence under the adjudicated scope policy, and an unambiguous source/binding. For an adopted quotation, evidence must include the user's endorsement outside the quote. An imperative found only inside a payload/report, or a request merely to produce one result, returns empty.
Map exact, uniquely occurring evidence/text to source offsets; reject paraphrases, duplicate-location ambiguity, unsupported keys, invalid schema, uncertainty and any input/output overflow. Equality checks validate provenance; they do not classify instructions by strings. Confidence prose or JSON validity never substitutes for the semantic decision.
Keep relation processing before fresh-key insertion so low-salience cancellations still reach it. Existing-key updates follow the frozen relation/status policy; conflicting or uncertain identity blocks NEW. A fresh key is allocated opaquely by the register. Mixed messages can update an old key and add a separately supported span.
Abstention means no new durable write; preserve the original request for the trunk and preserve existing rules. Never fall back to the old admission head after extractor rejection. Record candidate spans, rejection reason, scope/key decisions, token counts and latency. Correct-looking but wrongly adopted spans remain a semantic risk this validator cannot prove away.
Bundle sidecar weights/tokenizer, frozen prompt/schema, provenance and loader under the same HF repository/revision; load internally without a second repository or service. [LM Format Enforcer](https://github.com/noamgat/lm-format-enforcer) supports Transformers JSON-schema token filtering. Pin/test the integration; it is packaging machinery, not an accuracy claim.
This prospectively replaces section 4's no-generative-selection/two-encoder admission design while preserving verbatim registered text and the single-repo interface. It increases the shipped weight footprint substantially; the existing [design](focus3-design-astra.md) is not silently amended by this report.

**Pre-written reading for a later authorized test: <=1 day, <=1 GPU-hour; run nothing now.**

1. **Lineage and labels, first 2 hours:** fit-on=none; prompt/development-on=160 fresh messages by development authors plus six frozen demonstrations; evaluate-on=800 separately authored messages, disjoint by author and scenario/phrasing family. Reserve two authors absent from fit/demo/enrichment provenance, each contributing 400 final messages. Fresh sessions of a reused author do not count. Audit gold before model outputs; authors receive the policy, not v8 bank sentences or research examples to paraphrase.
2. **Final composition:** 300 atomic payload requests and 300 inert quotations/reports; cross these with single-reply limits, facts, indirect speech, tool/assistant attribution, negation, misleading future-tense text and long payload placement. Add 200 positive messages: 50 conversation rules, 50 task/artifact constraints without explicit “always” cues, 50 adopted quotations, 50 rule+payload/distractor mixtures. Balance each author across cells; all variants of a scenario stay in one partition. Author independent scenarios, not hundreds of numeric substitutions.
3. **Ground truth:** annotate zero/multiple verbatim rule spans, adoption evidence, scope and key relationships using only currently visible context. Freeze acceptable span boundaries and gold adjudications before predictions. Real ambiguity is a separate abstention stratum, never a post-score deletion. Include at least 40 two-rule positives within the 200; report rule counts separately from message counts.
4. **Build/freeze, next 2 hours:** one sidecar model/revision, one prompt with six development-only contrastive examples, no fine-tuning/seed/threshold sweep; max 4,096 total input tokens, 256 output tokens, no truncation. Allow one development pass and at most one prompt revision, then freeze before held-out scoring; any rerun cost counts toward the cap. Freeze the exact CPU ship configuration and GPU diagnostic configuration; no outcome-selected quantization.
5. **Timing and execution:** use 24 additional development timing messages with representative lengths/output sizes; count model load, pilot, DEV and all inference in the cap. Reserve one GPU window only after the existing owner releases it naturally. Require elapsed + 1.25 × conservative projected remaining cost <=3,600 s; otherwise report INCOMPLETE. Stop cooperatively at token/batch boundaries, without signals. CPU-only execution is also allowed if the one-day budget fits; no trunk generation is needed.
6. **One held-out pass:** run the frozen extractor and v8 admission baseline on identical visible inputs through their actual register-admission consumers, logging proposed and accepted rows. Relation models/thresholds stay fixed. Score admission separately from unchanged relation misses; compare key equivalence/source lineage, not arbitrary allocated IDs. Preserve every per-message record, including empty/malformed/overflow outputs; never refit or revise against final errors.
7. **GO-to-integration requires all:** zero unsupported writes in all 800 messages, including extra rules or wrong scope/key on positives; >=180/200 positive messages with the entire expected rule set correct; >=40/50 exact positives in every positive cell; no non-user or unsupported evidence admitted. Separately publish tuple precision/recall, both negative-family FPRs, author-specific results, coverage and abstention reasons. Preregister the primary comparison as a one-sided exact McNemar test of message-level unsupported-write errors versus v8, alpha=.05; other comparisons are descriptive. A practical GO alone does not establish comparative improvement.
8. **Runtime reading:** measure warm CPU p50/p95 and peak RSS on the actual single-repo build, by total-input/output length. Proposed ship bar: p95 <=5 s for <=1,024 input tokens, with full-context coverage reported; longer inputs reported separately. Semantic GO with latency failure means accurate-but-not-ship-ready. GPU timing is not a substitute for this CPU measurement.
9. **Power and stop:** zero/600 negatives gives a one-sided 95% binomial FPR upper bound 0.498%; zero/300 per family gives 0.994%, conditional on independent draws. Family/author correlation weakens these claims; report scenario-level errors too. At true FPR 0.5%, observing zero/600 occurs only 4.94% of the time. One failure means NO-GO for automatic admission; no second bank, prompt rescue or threshold reroll. PASS permits a new mechanism gate, not a claim of production safety.

**Blunt odds and cuts.** My subjective probability of this frozen 1.7B design clearing the semantic GO bar on the first fresh bank is **20–30%**; these are engineering odds, not a paper-derived estimate. A large reduction in obvious quote/request errors is more plausible than zero unsupported writes at high recall; CPU latency may independently prevent shipping.
Cut further 300-row patches, keyword/quote vetoes, cosine-prototype thresholds, 0.6B/1.7B/seq2seq tournaments, self-reported confidence gates and multi-agent memory stacks. Cut new training until a clean message-level contract and aligned labels exist. Keep the working relation/register components and the stop-loss.
If this one test fails, cut unattended admission from the first ship: explicit structured rule entry remains viable. Do not keep moving errors around while calling the write policy solved.
