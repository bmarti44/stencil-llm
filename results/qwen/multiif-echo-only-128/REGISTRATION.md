# Exp 1 registration: pin increment given echo (Multi-IF, Qwen3-1.7B)

Registered 2026-09-11 before any GPU spend (plan/BACK-ON-TRACK-PLAN.md, section B,
Exp 1). Post-hoc ablation on an EXPOSED cohort: the 909 Multi-IF records under
`results/qwen/multiif-evict-909-prequery-v2/` were generated and analysed before this
registration; the two new arms below are added to a deterministic 128-source subset.

## Question

Once the selected prior-user text is echoed into the final user turn, do the selected
KV pins add anything? And does the zero-parameter arm we would ship (role/recency
sentences, standalone token budget, no pins) hold up against the classifier arms?

## Data lineage

- Evaluated on: `data/bench/multiif_en.jsonl` (909 conversations, English), through
  the saved contexts in the exposed records (`context_token_ids`,
  `echo_context_token_ids`, `evict_range`, `classifier_spans`, `selector_candidates`).
- Fit on: nothing. No parameter, threshold, or budget is chosen with any outcome from
  this benchmark. The sentence classifier is the frozen `data/classifier/model/ft`
  (published as `bmarti44/assistant-memory-sentence-classifier`), trained on
  model-written sentences with no benchmark content (`data/classifier/LABELS.md`).
- Disjointness: evaluated-on and fit-on are disjoint by construction.

## Unit and sampling

- Unit: SOURCE prompt. The 909 conversations are 484 distinct first-user-prompt
  sources (425 pairs + 59 singletons sharing their first message). Source id =
  sha256 of the first user message text decoded from `context_token_ids`.
- Selection: sort the 484 source ids, draw 128 with `random.Random(0).sample`, and
  take the conversation with the lowest `ci` per chosen source. No outcome is read
  during selection. The selection is written to `selection.json` before any run.

## Arms (same saved histories, same eviction coordinates, `max_new=512`, `deadline=300 s`, greedy)

Existing arms reused from the exposed records: `full`, `evicted`, `clf_pinned`,
`clf_pinned_echo`, `role_pinned`.

New arms, generated by `scripts/multiif_echo_only.py`:

- `clf_echo_only`: `run_arm(echo_context_token_ids, evict_range=saved, keep=[])`.
  The classifier-selected sentences are echoed exactly as in `clf_pinned_echo`
  (same `echo_context_token_ids`), with zero pinned columns.
- `role_echo_only`: prior-user SENTENCES (the saved `selector_candidates`, which are
  every sentence of every prior user turn) selected newest-first (by turn, then by
  span start, descending) until the rendered ledger would exceed E = 256 tokens
  INCLUDING the header line `Earlier user instructions restated verbatim:`; the
  walk stops at the first sentence that does not fit (no skipping ahead to older,
  shorter sentences, so recency is never violated; a conversation whose newest
  prior sentence alone exceeds E gets an empty echo, and that count is reported); the
  chosen sentences are rendered in chronological order by `render_text_ledger` and
  inserted by `text_ledger_context` (identical renderer and insertion point as the
  classifier echo); eviction coordinates must equal the saved `evict_range` (assert),
  zero pinned columns. The budget is standalone: it is not borrowed from the
  classifier's pin count.

## Compatibility check (before the 128-run)

Replay `clf_pinned_echo` for the first 8 selected conversations on the current
runtime (`run_arm(echo_context_token_ids, evict_range, keep=classifier_spans)`).
Require identical `generated_token_ids` on 8/8; otherwise the compared existing arms
(`clf_pinned_echo`, `role_pinned`, `full`) are regenerated on the 128 subset and the
new runtime is used throughout (documented in RESULTS.md); runtimes are never mixed.

## Scoring

`_score_fields(row, last_turn, text)` from `scripts/multiif_evict.py`, with the
instruction ids and kwargs looked up from `data/bench/multiif_en.jsonl` by `ci`,
exactly as the original run. Per-conversation aged-instruction rate = 100 × mean of
the `aged` bits. Safety fields: `timed_out`, `truncated`, `degenerate`
(`repetition_4gram > 0.5` or truncated), `invalid` (`invalid_output`).

## Estimands (source-level paired differences in aged-instruction points)

- D1 = clf_pinned_echo − clf_echo_only (the pin increment given echo)
- D2 = role_pinned − role_echo_only (pins vs echo at the role rule)
- D3 = role_echo_only − clf_echo_only (which selector the text package defaults to)

For each: paired mean over the 128 sources; 95% percentile bootstrap interval over
sources (10,000 resamples, `numpy.random.default_rng(0)`); exact two-sided sign test
on discordant sources (sources where the two arms' aged rates differ), with the
discordant count reported. Noninferiority margin: 2 points.

Safety: per arm, counts of invalid / degenerate / truncated / timed-out outputs as
EXCESS over `full` on the same 128 conversations; reported, no integer kill rule.

## Readings (exhaustive, fixed before launch)

- D1 interval entirely above +2 → pins earn their place; Release 2 keeps the KV
  backend option.
- D1 interval entirely below +2 → pins are dispensable at this margin; Release 2 is
  the text-only package.
- D1 interval straddles +2 → insufficient evidence at n = 128; Release 2 is text-only
  on cost grounds, stated as such.
- D3: interval entirely above 0 → role rule is the default selector; entirely below
  0 → classifier is the default; otherwise the role rule is the default on cost
  grounds (zero parameters) and the classifier is offered as an option.
- D2 is descriptive.
- Demonstrated harm (an interval entirely below 0 for D1 or D2, i.e. pins hurt) is
  reported as harm, never as inconclusive.

## Budget

Filled in from the Exp 0 timing pilot before launch: seconds per conversation for
two 512-token generations at the maximum saved context length, × 128, × 1.5
contention factor. Recorded here as `BUDGET:` line by the launch commit. Reservation
through `tools/gpu_reserve.sh exp1 <minutes> <peak-gb>`.

## Artifacts

`results/qwen/multiif-echo-only-128/`: `selection.json`, `replay.json`,
`conv-<ci>.json` (schema 2, atomic, written as each conversation completes),
`summary.json`, `RESULTS.md`, `manifest.json` (sha256 of every record).

## Stopping

`--start/--limit` chunks of at most 50 minutes; a run stops starting new
conversations when its reservation has 5 minutes left; incomplete runs resume by
missing `ci`. Budget exhaustion is recorded INCOMPLETE, never rescued.
