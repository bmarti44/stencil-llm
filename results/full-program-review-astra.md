# Full-program audit — gpt-6-astra, 2026-09-07

**Verdict: the program supports a bounded explicit-state rendering experiment and several useful negative engineering results. It does not yet support a generalized focus mechanism, a complete downloadable product, reliable automatic updating, or a claim that weight-based control is impossible. The strongest recurring defect is a valid count attached to an invalid causal interpretation. I made that mistake repeatedly. Reviews caught it, but append-only corrections often left the misleading headline intact.**

Audit baseline: `eb8270c7a5e18bb0dd093bf2cc7a805e24c6b505`. CPU only. No model inference, GPU query, process signal, container operation, benchmark-directory access, or evaluation-bank episode-content access. Classifier held-out access was restricted to first-line summary metadata. The larger test's registration was read; its checkpoints, outputs, journals and episode contents were not. The flag named in the request, `results/larger-test/RUNNING.flag`, was absent; the registration actually specifies `results/quick-checks/larger-test/RUNNING.flag` (`results/larger-test/REGISTRATION.md:21`). Absence of the former is not permission to use the GPU. Only this report is written; the user’s single-file instruction overrides historical ledger-writing requirements.

Evidence conventions: paths are repository-relative; `path:line` identifies the supporting passage. **SUPPORTED** means supported at the stated population, denominator and measurement level, not guaranteed to reproduce on new generations. **OVERSTATED** means a real measurement is extended beyond its identification or uncertainty. **UNSUPPORTED** means the required measurement is absent. **CONTRADICTED** means the records, current code or another explicit receipt establish the opposite. Duplicate claims across index/report/review are grouped. Registered thresholds and proposals are distinguished from achieved outcomes. Prior reviewers' record replays are evidence attributed to those reviewers; I do not relabel their work as my own re-execution.

I independently compared the pilot-6/7 saved DEV HTTP requests/completions, recomputed the delivery sign-test sensitivity and both cost formulas, inspected the current register/renderer/loop/journal/SLAB-2/endpoint/driver/package code, and ran two in-memory CPU counterexamples against the current controller. No broad test suite was run: some legacy tests instantiate evaluation banks, and this audit must not do that.

## 2. What would not replicate

**The measured starting point is156/166=93.9759% completion identity across two containers, not deterministic greedy generation.** I reproduced it from `composition-pilot-{6,7}/local/http/main/` by matching full request dictionaries and comparing text,token IDs,finish reason. N differs4/95,T6/71;R/Q requests changed and supply no matched sample. The published cell Wilson interval3.3–10.7% for divergence ignores clustering; the current registration explicitly acknowledges that (`results/larger-test/REGISTRATION.md:7`). It is not a6.02pp confidence band on every score and cannot be transferred to other models, check40k, or LoRA training.

Ranked replication risks:

1. **Pilot7's compact gate outcome and one-cell arm rankings — high.** R4/34 versusN3/34 differs by one output but represents the same1/8 episode rate. N moved0/34→3/34 without an N-facing code change. R final3/8 versusN/T2/8, format7/8 versus8/8, and R-delivery8/8 versusT7/8 are all one-episode distinctions. A single onset change can lock four to sixteen later rounds. Do not advertise these gaps as stable treatment rankings.
2. **Pilot7 DEV significance — high fragility, not refuted evidence.** Seven wins/zero losses gives Holm3/128=.0234375. If one win becomes a tie, six/zero gives.046875; two ties give.09375. If one win becomes a loss, six/one gives3×8/128=.1875. These are exact sign-count sensitivities, not an assumed stochastic model. The seven-episode delivery direction is larger than the one-cell compact difference, but its exact eight-episode significance is not robust to one reversed paired result. It needs the registered independent64, not another DEV rescue.
3. **Pilot5's execution and expansion factors — high.**65 R nonwrites are mostly four locked lanes; changing one first reply changes up to16 rows. Independent reruns need not preserve either the aggregate ranking or the nominal factor.
