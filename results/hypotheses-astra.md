**Five breakthrough hypotheses — astra, 2026-09-05**

The best odds are a small stateful controller using an address the frozen model already understands. Establish that cheap bridge first; then test whether control can move entirely inside the trunk. Another large dense-vector sweep has poor odds. These are ranked by subjective probability of passing a useful next screen, not calibrated forecasts or probabilities of solving long coding sessions; none establishes that final goal.
Scope: proposals only, CPU analysis and live primary-source research; no model/GPU process launched. This file is the only authorized write. Historical protocol/state were read under `archive/plan/`; the brief supersedes their ledger-write requirement. These proposals neither register nor modify FOCUS-1 or its current probe.

**1. Native text-address routing with an external focus latch — 65% pass; cap 2 GPU-h**

Hypothesis: a persistent A/B/OFF latch, rendered as a short slot address into an unchanged skill-description bank, can provide reliable set/hold/switch/clear through the frozen model's native instruction interface.
Why here: SELECTOR already demonstrated that a tiny address can govern a frozen real trunk; checks 28/30 show the cost of forcing activation magnitude. Native routing avoids their actuator failure. This has the highest engineering odds and the lowest scientific novelty: the bank contains task descriptions, and the model interprets a textual address. It is an engineering focus interface, not evidence of an internal mobile stencil.
QUICK TEST: place both skill descriptions once in a fixed bank; a deterministic oracle latch emits only `slot 1`, `slot 2`, or `OFF` at each decision. No task sentence is repasted and no answer enters the state. Randomize bank order and slot-to-skill assignment across episodes. Compare P's arms, full-task-text reminder and a slot-only arm with the bank removed. Reuse SELECTOR prompt construction and FOCUS-1's short decoding/scoring plumbing; no fitting or dose grid.
Measurement/reading fixed now: require P, and exact episode success at least 90% of the full-text reminder's count, with no extra broken episodes relative to it. Repeat on a separately seeded 32-episode bank for upper/lower ASCII case conversion, with eight mixed-case letters and exact JSON-string output, after the same admission thresholds; BOTH banks must pass. Use distinct `purpose` suffixes for this bank's admission/setup/test. Count bank, address and reminder tokens separately; this small bank does not promise a cost saving.
Rules out: default sorting, answer memorization, slot-number/position shortcuts, and a single skill-pair accident. The missing-bank arm quantifies dependence on descriptions; success with the bank is ordinary conditional prompting, which remains the disclosed explanation. HOLD resides in the latch; CLEAR is tested on retained history, not by resetting the model.
A pass unlocks #2 and a strong baseline for later coding sessions with cancellations and compactions. It does not justify claiming that text routing itself is a novel breakthrough.

**2. A normalized recurrent sidecar can learn the focus state from completion costs — 40% pass; cap 4 GPU-h**

Hypothesis: separately normalized input/state channels plus learning from downstream completion costs will produce a causal, transplantable task state, where the old wave's numerically suppressed recurrence did not.
Why here: the [wave report](internal-wave-report.md) found |h20|≈609 versus |state|≈6 and saturated gain logits; completion-loss training beat the actuator-identical proxy. The [plan](../INTERNAL-WAVE-PLAN.md) also exposed a task that reserialized authority, making memory unnecessary. Fix those two concrete defects; do not add an oscillator. [Guiding Giants (2025)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.20309) supports lightweight frozen-model controllers, but does not demonstrate this temporal claim.
QUICK TEST: use #1's frozen address interface only if its setup ceiling passes; otherwise mark this screen INELIGIBLE. Adapt `WaveRNN`: RMS-normalize h20 and the 64-D recurrent state separately; read operand-free set/switch/clear/no-change event text, and emit only hard A/B/OFF. Past event text is unavailable to execution and to later controller calls; state is the sole carry. Train on 128 synthetic event episodes, cycling through the 128 fit operand sets, randomized hold lengths 1–4 and initial addresses; test holds 5–8 and separately frozen event phrasings. Current operands never enter the controller.
Cheap behavioral objective: cache frozen-trunk canonical-completion CE for each of the three candidate addresses on fit rows, then minimize sum_j p_j·CE_j with BPTT on the controller; Adam 1e-3, 20 epochs, final checkpoint, three seeds. This reuses the wave's completion-cost principle, NOT its differentiable attention actuator; inference uses argmax, whose performance must be measured. Cache features/costs once; fit the small controller on CPU. Require finite nonzero CE-only gradients and a detached-probability negative control.
Measurement/reading: require P in every seed and ≥95% held-event address accuracy, including CLEAR. At matched HOLD decisions, resetting state must reduce exact task success by ≥12/32 with McNemar p≤.01; transplanting the state from an opposite-task, different-operand donor must yield donor-task answers on ≥24/32 recipients. A capacity-matched stateless controller and a latch using the same event classifier are mandatory controls; the recurrent arm must beat stateless by ≥12 episodes/p≤.01 and finish within two successful episodes of the latch. No requirement to beat a perfect oracle latch.
Rules out: persistent cue text, operand computation in the controller, decorative recurrence and lucky initialization. A latch tie establishes learned state sufficiency, not superiority or a need for oscillation. A pass unlocks fresh natural-language scope/cancellation events and a small executed-coding pilot; it does not establish autonomous relevance discovery.

**3. Replace a task-discriminative coordinate instead of adding a common instruction vector — 30% pass; cap 2 GPU-h**

Hypothesis: bounded replacement along the A-versus-B activation contrast can select the task while avoiding the shared formatting/amplification component that dominates task-versus-OFF vectors.
Why here: check 31's nearly parallel vectors and check 30's sustained-injection truncations fit this diagnosis, but do not prove it. [GAPS, September 2026 preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.01878v1), reports better behavior/capability trade-offs from selective dimension conditioning on Qwen3-1.7B and Gemma-3; that modestly raises this rank. Its concept-suppression results are not evidence for skill switching, and the test below is an inference from its selectivity principle, not a replication.
QUICK TEST: extract operand-paired mean final-prompt states μ_A, μ_B on the 128 fit lists. At layer input L, let u=(μ_A−μ_B)/||μ_A−μ_B|| and c_t=u·μ_t; replace h with h+clip(c_t−u·h,−d,d)u, d=|c_A−c_B|. Apply ONLY at the final prompt token; decoding is unhooked. OFF is identity. Test L=12,16,20 in that order on setup; select the first with ≥15/16 exact immediate answers per task and zero broken outputs, else FAIL-ACTUATOR. Freeze once; reuse `mean_difference`, `residual_hook` and cache helpers.
Measurement/reading: require P on fresh test episodes; additionally compare a fixed random-unit direction and the common-mode direction normalize(μ_A+μ_B−2μ_OFF), each multiplied by the same signed scalar displacement recorded from the correct run at that prompt position. Each control must lose ≥12 whole episodes to the correct intervention with McNemar p≤.01. Zero/nonfinite directions are INVALID. Report the original sustained task-versus-OFF recipe descriptively on setup only; do not select among methods using test outcomes.
Rules out: generic activation energy, stronger JSON formatting, a constant answer-token push and direct answer transport: A/B/copy contain the same fresh operands in different orders. This still does not localize a stored circuit, and pulse-only failure would reject this actuator, not all steering. A pass unlocks a second unrelated skill pair and an address controller; retained-cache CLEAR remains binding despite the hook already being off.

**4. A small operand-free KV packet can carry and transplant the active task — 20% pass; cap 2 GPU-h**

Hypothesis: task state is easier to write once into a few persistent KV columns than to sustain through repeated residual additions, and replacing those columns can switch and clear it.
Why here: [toy transplant](../archive/README.md) supports the causal question but used trained toy wiring; the real wave never reached its transplant rung. [KV Cache Steering (2025)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.08799v2) reports one-shot control and reasoning-style transfer in frozen LLMs. This raises the chance of persistence, not of clean release; cached downstream contamination makes this a long shot.
QUICK TEST: from 32 newly authored, operand/answer-free task-cue paraphrases per A/B/OFF, prefill the same four-token neutral suffix at identical absolute positions. Average only suffix K/V at layers 12–27 to make three frozen packets. The recipient has those same neutral suffix tokens/positions; SET replaces only their four columns, keeping all other K/V identical. No donor cue-token columns are copied. SWITCH/BACK overwrite the same slots with the other/initial packet; CLEAR restores the OFF packet without rebuilding any other cache. Use `KVCache`, pre-query ordering and `clone_history`; no training, grid or full-prefix transplant.
Measurement/reading: require P, plus an actual retained-SET HOLD fork: append 128 neutral tokens and fresh operands with NO further packet write or hook. At least 24/32 must follow the original task, beating its exact-token-history OFF-packet replay by ≥12 episodes/p≤.01. Opposite-donor packet transfer must pass P's donor-target test; a same-size OFF-donor sham controls cache surgery. Record per-layer cache residuals and both CLEAR replies; a mere successful cache deletion is not successful clearance.
Rules out: donor-answer leakage, copied instruction-token KV, fresh reapplication masquerading as hold, and prior generated text alone explaining persistence. The fixed packet may still encode a latent task instruction: success would establish transferable task state, not content-free neural circuitry. A pass unlocks packet-size reduction and learned writes; a CLEAR failure closes this tested mechanism despite a good SET score.

**5. Coarse multiplicative MLP gates can route existing skills without adding vectors — 15% pass; cap 4 GPU-h**

Hypothesis: selecting among bounded gain patterns over existing MLP pathways can activate the appropriate frozen competency more safely than adding new residual content.
Why here: this targets the storage/control split directly, but the [GPT-2 gate oracle](gpt2-report.md) failed 0/8 while additive injection worked; that is adverse evidence. Its competence/content-memory setting differs from this admitted skill pair. The internal-wave positive motivates completion-loss optimization, not confidence that this new actuator will work. This is the longest shot, not an excuse to revive blind attention boosting.
QUICK TEST: attach a pre-hook to `down_proj` in Qwen layers 20–27; multiply its existing MLP input by 32 contiguous-group gains per layer, independently for A/B. There are 256 scalars per skill; g=.5+.5·sigmoid(z), z initialized at 4 plus N(0,.01²), OFF bypasses the hook. No residual addition, attention-column bias, LoRA or trunk-weight update. Optimize canonical CE+.01·mean((1−g)²) for 200 Adam steps at 1e-2, batch 4, on the 128 fit lists; three init seeds, final checkpoints, no layer/rank/dose search. Apply selected gains at final prompt and decoding positions only.
Measurement/reading: require P in every seed, with finite nonzero CE-only gate gradients, detached-gate failure and bitwise OFF identity on matched inputs. A fixed within-layer group permutation of learned gains and uniform gains with the same per-layer mean must each lose ≥12 whole episodes/p≤.01. Reuse Qwen module hooks and the `w0_train.py` gradient-battery recipe by reading/adapting functions, never importing that script. Admission and frozen trunk hashes are mandatory.
Rules out: arbitrary additive content, generic suppression, dead gradients and best-seed reporting. It cannot fully rule out the learned gate bank itself acquiring task-specific computation; coarse capacity and pretrained admission only reduce that concern. A pass unlocks causal pathway localization and gate transfer to new task descriptions/operands before any larger controller.
