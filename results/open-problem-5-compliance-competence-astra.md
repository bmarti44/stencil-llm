# Compliance without competence: can internal focus be salvaged?

2026-09-07 · gpt-6-astra · Open problem 5 · research and proposed experiments only.

**Brian's intended mechanism was focus, not memory. That was a reasonable research idea. The unsupported step was treating a movable language/format preference as a selectable, competence-preserving skill. The evidence favors sparse, conditional control over an already competent computation; it does not favor another unrestricted attention or router-dose search. The cheapest informative successor is a decision-boundary pulse using the existing check49 adapter, with ordinary current-rule rendering as the competence comparator, on a new sealed task bank.**

Accordingly, “wrong idea” is too strong; “right idea, merely executed badly” is also too forgiving. The original focus objective survives, while the generic skill-knob interpretation does not yet have evidence.

**What the literature calls the problem, and what might cause it.** I found no established technical term precisely matching “compliance without competence.” The studied terms are **oversteering**, **preference–utility tradeoff**, and instruction-following versus task-fidelity tradeoffs. These describe an empirical failure pattern, not a theorem that focus cannot improve reasoning.

[DIRECTER, ICLR2026, Table1 and §3.2](https://arxiv.org/html/2603.06745v1): Llama-3.1-8B baseline format/task79.2/82.7; PASTA99.2/48.1; tuned PASTA98.9/62.7; SpotLight98.8/38.0; tuned SpotLight95.4/78.7; DIRECTER99.1/86.9. Default/tuned PASTA suppress noninstruction attention by .01/.1; SpotLight target mass .3/.1. DIRECTER scales instruction keys by100, uses beta=.5 plausibility, progressively removes steering layers when the candidate token has insufficient baseline probability, and can fall back to baseline. Thus dynamic attention mass alone is insufficient. Few-shot prompting already gets98.9/87.1. These are math answers under formatting constraints, not executable code. Its LIFBench evidence concerns long-context instructions, not switch/release trajectories; reported throughput loss is about16%. There is no theorem that baseline-plausible tokens preserve semantics. This is the strongest directly paired format-plus-verifiable-answer positive found, with an ordinary prompting comparator that remains competitive.

[AxBench, Table2 and §5](https://arxiv.org/html/2501.17148): at the earlier reported layers, prompting/ReFT-r1 scores are .698/.633 on Gemma-2-2B and1.075/.630 on9B; the gap grows .065→.445. SAE scores .177/.191. Scores combine LLM-judged concept presence, instruction relevance, and fluency, not program execution. Prompting uses the interface instruction tuning trained; a good detector need not be a good intervention. This supports the local skepticism about named skill vectors, not a universal scaling law. A [May2026 SAE re-evaluation](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.31183v1) improves steering by changing feature labeling but explicitly still does not match prompting on AxBench; its title is not evidence that the prompting gap disappeared.

Mechanistically, a low-rank edit is small in parameter count, not necessarily small in computational effect. If a query changes by delta-q, the attention log-odds between two fixed keys change by `delta-q · (k_i − k_j) / sqrt(d)`. One direction aligned with important key differences can reroute evidence everywhere. Router perturbations additionally change which expert computations contribute. This algebra explains a possible failure mechanism; Stencil has not measured which lost40k task computation it disrupted.

[SKOP, May2026, §§4–5/Table1](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.06342v1) identifies focus-to-tail attention rerouting and projects query edits away from dominant utility-critical key-difference directions, protecting selected high-risk heads. Its Llama-3.1-8B baseline/CAA/SKOP GSM8K accuracy is75.2/14.0/66.6; IFBench26.2/14.5/25.0. That is much less damage, **not full competence preservation**. Its 5–7× degradation reduction and >95% efficacy headline must retain that distinction. Default calibration uses4,000 examples; an ablation reports substantial recovery with250. Long-context evaluation adopts simple single-needle retrieval, not the whole RULER task suite. Its calibration recipe draws on benchmark datasets and is not eligible for our fitting. Projection is a useful design principle, not a certified utility nullspace on arbitrary future inputs.

[Why Steering Works, February2026, §4](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.02343v1) explicitly posits a valid-generation manifold and steering-induced validity decay, then fits preference/utility curves and proposes a joint utility-preserving objective, SPLIT. The manifold mechanism is an assumption-backed model with empirical fits, not a measured causal diagnosis of our failures. Norm preservation alone cannot ensure staying on a curved manifold or retaining the task-relevant directions. A wrong answer can remain fluent and distributionally plausible.

“Surface features are cheaper to move than reasoning” is a useful **inference**, not a demonstrated universal separation. Format/language often turns on a few output decisions; a program must satisfy many coupled semantic constraints. Our proxy-directed optimization rewards the former without necessarily protecting the latter. An [SAE code-correctness study, October2025, §5.2](https://arxiv.org/html/2510.02917v1) reports4.04% repair of initially wrong programs but14.66% corruption of initially correct programs at selected alpha29. Those have different denominators and cannot be subtracted as a net accuracy change. The study's proposed selective error-triggered use is a remedy hypothesis, not an already demonstrated zero-harm controller.

**What the better methods actually do differently.**

[PASTA, ICLR2024, Table1](https://arxiv.org/html/2311.02262) selects a subset of heads rather than perturbing every head indiscriminately. On LLaMA-7B, JSON format/profession accuracy rises60.00/54.94→96.64/85.09 with its multi-task configuration. Yet task-agnostic PASTA gives88.16/49.08: its own paper contains both joint improvement and a format/task tradeoff. Head selection and evaluation domain matter. These are structured classification/knowledge tasks, not executable coding or lifecycle evidence; the later DIRECTER comparison need not contradict the original study.

[SpotLight, EACL2026](https://aclanthology.org/2026.eacl-long.174/), with the opened [paper tables and §3](https://arxiv.org/html/2505.12025), intervenes when attention to an instruction span falls below a target. Qwen2.5-7B IFEval prompt/instruction .47/.59→.54/.66; HotpotQA exact-match/F1 .52/.66→.58/.72. Those are separate datasets, not paired compliance and semantic outcomes on the same coding tasks. ComplexBench structural gains are stronger than utility gains; the authors report no utility-constraint improvement. Its five-turn MT-IFEval appends generated responses and reduces reported average adherence drop18.2%→9.3%. This is multi-turn evidence, but not executable trajectory quality or rule-switch/release certification. Its own excessive-dose example becomes incoherent. The remedy is context-sensitive dose, with limits.

[ReCoVeR, EMNLP2025, §3/Table1](https://arxiv.org/html/2509.14814v1) controls natural language using parallel texts to separate language representation from shared content, rather than subtracting arbitrarily different answers. On Qwen2.5-7B MultiQ, adherence/accuracy90.7/61.8→92.0/62.7 for ReCoVeR, and93.2/66.5 for supervised ReCoVeR+. Unsupervised ReCoVeR is not uniformly positive: Gemma2 adherence/accuracy91.8/38.5→91.6/38.3. Supervision and content matching matter. These are QA/language results, not Python/JS execution or persistent switching. The supervised plus variant must not be advertised as a no-training control.

[Distribution-aware steering, April2026](https://arxiv.org/html/2604.08169v1), uses target/mirror projections relative to a learned boundary and intervenes only below thresholds, instead of adding a constant at every token. It reports better capability preservation on MMLU/MT-Bench/AlpacaEval and less repetition in five-/ten-turn conversations on70B/32B models. These are primarily trait/coherence studies, not same-task executable coding comparisons. [CAST, ICLR2025](https://arxiv.org/html/2409.05907v3), supplies the general condition-vector/behavior-vector decomposition for selective refusal; ordinary responses on other categories do not certify preserved programming competence.

[ROAST, February2026, Table2/AppendixA](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.14143v1), constructs directions from the model's own rollouts and uses continuous state steering and grouped normalization. It reports +9.66 pp GSM8K on Qwen3-0.6B and evaluates IFEval separately. This addresses mismatch between forced reference trajectories and actual model states, but does not establish paired coding compliance/quality. Its steering/development data are benchmark-derived; we cannot import that recipe's data under Stencil's lineage rule. [PARIF, ACL2026](https://aclanthology.org/2026.acl-long.1136.pdf), explicitly couples instruction rewards with correctness and reasoning-answer consistency using curriculum RL. That is evidence that the two objectives need not be intrinsically incompatible, but its26,000-example training program is not a one-hour frozen-trunk attention patch.

The common design improvement is to preserve the computation doing the task: select intervention sites, separate content from control, condition on an actual deficit, measure utility while selecting a dose, and keep a fallback. Neither a low-dimensional edit nor a detector score supplies that preservation by itself.

**The long-horizon evidence is no longer only single-turn.**

[TACT, May2026, §§2–3/Table1/AppendixC](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.05980v1), is the most relevant new positive: Qwen3.5-27B SWE-bench Verified resolve67.0→73.3%, Terminal-Bench2.0 30.3→36.0%; steps-to-resolve75.2→65.3 and48.2→41.2. It learns overthinking/overacting axes from labeled trajectories, selects layers on validation, and gates correction at the reasoning/action boundary. The paper declares problem-disjoint axis-train/axis-val/eval splits. It does **not** separately demonstrate arbitrary user-rule adherence, switching, or release. CLAW's pass@3/rubric result is not the same endpoint as executable SWE resolution. This is preprint evidence, not locally replicated; exact split manifests and hook implementation remain unverified here. In particular, a hook described as firing on the forward pass “producing” an end-of-thought token needs careful causal/token-index implementation. We would use a fixed, known boundary in a small screen, not infer a future sampled token. This source changes the prior for agent control without licensing its benchmark-derived fitting data here.

[Tunable Tool-Call Rates, August2026, §2/Figure5](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.25198v1), reports live-search QA accuracy .29→.56 at roughly one search per question. On Qwen3-4B its effective site is a middle-to-late layer band, with layer22 used as an operating point. Crucial distinction: extraction reads the last prompt token, but its stated intervention applies at **every position**. It is a decision-control positive, not published proof that a one-token pulse suffices. More tool calls alone are not better coding, and this is no long coding lifecycle certificate.

[Present but Rescaled, July2026](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.09156v1), finds that an injected direction survives into agent representations (agent/chat ratios .83–1.16), while behavioral coupling changes by model and ReAct format. Its refusal transfer can amplify or attenuate despite full transcript re-encoding and matched-norm random controls. This supports measuring the actual deployment scaffold and distinguishes representational persistence from useful behavioral control. Its outcomes are refusal, not coding competence. A prefill transplant does not reproduce its sustained effect.

Taken together: long context, retained dialogue, real tools, and executable coding all have some evidence, but in **different studies and contracts**. No opened source demonstrates their full conjunction with externally changed rules and clean release. Control OFF cannot undo the model's earlier edits, tool side effects, or self-authored demonstrations. A long-horizon controller must reassess current state at each decision and keep its release/history policy explicit. A token-plausibility guard cannot substitute for testing the resulting artifact.
