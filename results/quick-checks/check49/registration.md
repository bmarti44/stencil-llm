## Five GO conditions, verbatim from Astra

1. Full records within 3,600 GPU-held seconds, including load, training/profile passes, selection, generation, state rebuilds, validation while holding the GPU, and cleanup. Deadline/missing records = INCOMPLETE; failed competence, assets, or cost preflight = INELIGIBLE. Neither is a scientific null.
2. At least **10/12 M episodes** pass all four active decisions jointly on language **and** executable semantics; cold-HOLD ≥10/12. M exceeds X by ≥3/12 all-active episodes **and** the one-sided exact paired sign/McNemar test gives p≤.05. A 3-win/0-loss result has p=.125 and fails; five wins/zero losses gives .03125. This is a large-effect screen, not a powered search for small gains.
3. **No new executable failure or truncation** in M where the paired T request succeeds, including sentinels; report all paired losses instead of netting them against gains. M's all-active count must be at least T's. These strict observed-sample bars are not population noninferiority proofs; with 12 episodes even zero observed harms is weak evidence of rare-event safety.
4. CLEAR passes executable checks with the measured fresh OFF default in ≥11/12 episodes, and M adds **zero stale-mode impositions** relative to fresh OFF. Same-input OFF logits/token replay match the unmodified path under deterministic settings, with nonzero ON effects demonstrated. Exact parameter restoration and numerical parity are separate from behavioral release.

Shipping relevance: either more all-active executable successes than T, or at least equal success with ≥50% fewer *rule-carrier input positions* and no >10% increase in measured total inference time including rebuilds.
