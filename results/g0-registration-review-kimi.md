## Q2 — Oracle validity

**F2.1 [HIGH, unverified precondition] `evict()` position semantics.** The pasted excerpt does not include `evict()`. Required as pre-run tests, not assumptions: (i) surviving columns keep their **original** RoPE positions — eviction must not compact/re-pack, or every later token's relative geometry shifts and the oracle measures a position artifact; (ii) `restore` returns the cache to checksum-identical state ("restore the cache, evict exactly its columns"); (iii) teacher-forced reference tokens are appended at original positions; (iv) a span-text→KV-column mapping assertion on the **chat-templated** string with a pinned tokenizer revision (record in meta.json) — sentence-bounded spans on untemplated text vs templated columns is the classic silent off-by-one and would evict the wrong columns throughout.

**F2.2 [MEDIUM] Attention-sink confound not excluded at scan time.** Candidates are role-stratified incl. system/schema; nothing stops a candidate (or null) from including columns 0–3. Evicting the sink inflates NLL for geometric, not content, reasons. The registered "never-evictable" precondition covers the *arms*; the *oracle scan* needs the same exclusion (or separate reporting).

**F2.3 [MEDIUM] Self-distillation compresses the signal.** The chat reference is the base model's own greedy continuation — partially re-predictable after eviction → attenuated utilities, heavy mass at ~0 → null p90 can collapse to 0, making "fraction above p90" trivially trippable (interacts with F3.1). Tool references (rare-token JSON calls) will show inflated deltas. The two corpora are two different instruments sharing one threshold; also report utility stratified by copy-overlap with the reference.

**F2.4 [MEDIUM] Additive recovery over non-additive utilities.** The top-3 joint check diagnoses non-additivity (good), but `recovery` sums per-span positive utilities; negative-utility spans retained by a policy cost nothing (clipped), and an all-negative corpus yields 0/0. Arithmetic, clipping order (clip before vs after null subtraction), and zero-denominator behavior are unpinned — two readers can produce different tables.

Overall: teacher-forced counterfactual NLL is a legitimate label-free need proxy (KVzip-consistent), and signed utilities + position-matched nulls + joint replay are good practice. The defects are calibration/verification, not concept [LOW for the core idea].

## Q3 — Decision rules

**F3.1 [HIGH] The 0.10-above-null-p90 rule sits *on* the null expectation.** Recomputed: under H0 (candidates distributed as nulls), each candidate exceeds null p90 with p=0.10 by construction. With ≤12×30 = 360 candidates/corpus, sd ≈ √(360·0.09)=5.7, so P(fraction > 0.10 | H0) = P(X ≥ 37) ≈ **0.465 per corpus, ≈ 0.22 joint** — about one in five content-free pilots "finds signal," before within-dialogue clustering inflates it. And "null p90" has two live readings: per-dialogue (12 nulls → p90 ≈ the max/2nd-max order statistic; effectively undefined granularity) or corpus-pooled (mixing role/age/length buckets). This violates the "no outcome read two ways" standard. Fix: corpus-pooled, bucket-stratified null; a one-sided binomial or permutation test (p<0.05) with dialogue-cluster bootstrap; a pre-registered minimum effect (median candidate − matched-null > δ, δ pinned).

**F3.2 [HIGH] The 0.80 registration rule runs on point estimates at n=30, with winner's curse.** CIs are required only for G1's comparison, not for the decision that *skips* G1 and registers a selector. Five policies are argmax-selected on the same 30 dialogues, and the winner is "applied UNCHANGED to the gate benchmarks" directly — this contradicts the same paragraph's "readouts … gating only the NEXT step." Fix: select on the 30, **confirm on a fresh 70 from the stored ≤200** (dialogue-cluster bootstrap; pre-registered confirm floor), and only then register. Also pin: "both corpora" = per-corpus recovery each meeting the band (not pooled); band edges as written (`≥0.80`; `[0.50,0.80)`; `<0.50`) are consistent — keep.

**F3.3 [MEDIUM] B is defined two ways.** Registered: "all at one fixed budget B." Brief: "B = total tokens of the top-25% candidates" — per-dialogue, oracle-derived (≤3 of ≤12 spans ≈ 192–384 tokens), token-budget vs span-count semantics for policies unpinned, and undefined when top-25% utilities are ≤0. Pin one definition.

**F3.4 [LOW] Timing gates diverge across docs:** brief stops the pilot if projection >4 GPU-h; the registered ≤6 GPU-h gate is for the 1k launch; the synthesis estimates 1–3 GPU-h for 30+30. Pin referents.

## Q4 — Minimality and speed

At ≤28 forwards/dialogue (1 prefill + ≤24 evicted passes + 3 joints; ~1,680 total), 30+30 is close to minimal for what it's genuinely for: timing + signal + a *screen* of policies. As per F3.2 it is not — and should not pretend to be — decisive for policy registration; the registered text already says readouts gate only the next step, so make the decision rule match.

**F4.1 [MEDIUM] Last-turn-only references under-sample the motivating failure.** The oracle measures need for exactly one turn/dialogue, while the program's premise is *delayed/dormant* need (SCBench numbers cited in evidence; S2 is a gate benchmark). Recent+sinks or BM25 can "win" the wrong contest because OASST2/APIGen-MT final turns mostly concern recent content. Cost-free fix: report utility and fraction>p90 **by age bucket** (buckets already exist for the nulls); for the 1k stage, register ≥2 measured turns/dialogue.

**F4.2 [LOW] Cuts/adds.** Cut: joint check top-3→top-2 and, only if over the time budget, nulls 12→8 (keep 1:1 pairing). Do not add policies. Add (cheap, CPU): the 70-dialogue confirm stage; the decontamination record; the span↔column mapping test; pinned constants for policy (e) layers/heads and the BM25 tokenizer (both are researcher degrees of freedom that must be frozen before the gate run).

## Q5 — Generality claim

If a policy wins under the fixed design and is applied unchanged to Multi-IF/BFCL (+S2), the claim is exactly: **zero-shot transfer of a selector chosen solely on two disjoint public corpora, retaining a pre-registered fraction of label-free oracle utility, without any benchmark-derived fitting, at equal budget with protocol roles pinned** — versus null-matched and random-span controls. It may **not** claim: superiority over benchmark-fit selectors on their home turf (a fit-on-bench finder will likely still top it on Multi-IF; the claim is generality per training cost); equivalence of oracle recovery with task success (the gate must show Multi-IF compliance / BFCL AST-match deltas vs full-KV at the pinned budget — perfect G0 numbers with a degraded gate = failure); "waves select" or theory evidence (synthesis §6); generality beyond the 2+2 cells (APIGen-MT↔BFCL is one macro-family; OASST2↔Multi-IF is adjacent).

The model card must state: fit/select corpora + hashes + licenses + draw procedure (`data/g0/MANIFEST.json`); the decontamination method and result; policy (d)'s b3 lineage *including `mt-train-300`* wherever (d) appears, even as a comparison; the oracle's definition and the self-distilled chat reference; the select-30/confirm-70 split; that separation was enforced by `tests/test_eval_data_separation.py` + guard, and that this enforcement is name/path-based (necessary, not sufficient — hence the manifest audit); all gate floors and control results.
