Change rate by field: label 4/1234 (0.32%); `message_new_rule` 4/1234 (0.32%); `new_rule_spans` 7/1234 (0.57%); `target_span` text 1/1234 (0.08%); stray or misspelled metadata key 2/1234 (0.16%); deletions 3/1234 (0.24%). Systematic error 1 — offsets: `target_span.end` is wrong on 1,031/1,234 rows (`end != start + len(text)`) and `start` on 53. Both are fixable by rule (`start = message.find(text)`, then `end = start + len(text)`), so they are not itemised; row 214 is the only span whose text is not a verbatim substring at all and is itemised.
Systematic error 2 — splitting: 1,117/1,234 rows use the whole message as the candidate span, so multi-clause override messages are never sentence-split as the spec's splitter requires; the clause-level spans that do exist are exactly the ones with broken offsets.
Systematic error 3 — admitted spans: `new_rule_spans` emits three shapes (50 bare strings, 16 span objects, 5 flat `[start,end,text]` arrays at 754/766/775/778/784), and several string spans are sub-clause fragments ("postmortem reminder" 684, "add type hints" 115, "keep them in English" 924) rather than the verbatim persistent instruction.
Systematic error 4 — identity and schema: `id` and `scenario_id` are absent on every row and `author`/`session_id`/`split` on 805, so the spec's scenario-level split cannot be enforced — rows 130/131 are a deliberate one-message/two-target pair that must share a scenario group. Field naming also drifts (flat `old_rule`, `prev_user`, `new_rule_spans`), identically to kimi-transitions, so this is writer-level not row-level.
Content quality is high. Status preconditions are near-clean: 2 violations in 1,234 rows against 50 in kimi-relations. All 53 non-user-role rows (29 assistant, 24 tool) are `none` with `message_new_rule=false`, including 12 that assert a rule was cancelled or a task completed.
Every quoted, hedged, hypothetical and single-reply message carries `none`; `completes` never fires on a sub-unit closure (114, 343, 452, 497, 616, 781, 943, 1008) or a task switch; the v3 close-plus-promote combination is exercised correctly at 618 and 944.
